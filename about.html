Meet Our Founder

Theo Anold Gaopalelwe

Founder, CEO & CTO

Theo founded AXIOMIQ in August 2025 and leads the company's strategy, product, and engineering. He works with clients from the first conversation to the live system, designing, building, and launching the products himself.

His approach comes from everyday life in Botswana: watching friends, family, and small-business owners earn pula and end the month with nothing to show for it. He built PulaTrack so that money tools would finally be made for Batswana, by a Motswana.

Our Story

Built in Botswana, For Botswana

We can't build wealth in Botswana using tools that don't understand Botswana. The idea for AXIOMIQ came from watching money leave the country—not in corruption headlines, but in everyday life.

Friends, family, and small-business owners were earning pula and ending the month with nothing to show for it. The apps that existed were American, built for dollars and credit scores. So we decided to build our own.

Security First

We do not just build apps, we build secure apps. Data protection is part of every design decision.

Local Pride
